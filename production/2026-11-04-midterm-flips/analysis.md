# The 2026 Senate flips — fact table and packet plan

Brief (Kanchan, Nov 4, 2026): for each state that flipped, find what mattered most; check the street claims (farmers flipped Democrat; Texas, Kentucky, Maine and the Carolinas flipped); look at the Democratic Party's priorities and how it can serve the voters who moved, notably farmers. Objective, eight pieces like the Anthropic S-1 packet, each with images and a reel.

**All results are AP projections and unofficial counts as of Wednesday, Nov 4, 2026.** Recheck margins against certified results before any update.

## Street claims, checked

| Claim | Finding | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Texas flipped | **True.** Talarico (D) beat Paxton ~55–43; Hinojosa (D) beat Abbott for governor | Houston Public Media; NBC News |
| The Carolinas flipped | **True, both.** NC: Cooper 58.7–39.2. SC: Andrews 50.6–47.2 | WRAL/Carolina Public Press; PBS/WMBF |
| Maine went Democratic despite the Canada tariff waiver | **True.** Waiver signed Oct 23; Jackson beat Collins ~53.8–46.1 (86% in) | PBS/AP; CNBC; NBC Boston |
| Kentucky flipped | **False.** Andy Barr (R) beat Charles Booker (D) for the open McConnell seat | Spectrum News 1; NBC News |
| Farmers flipped Democrat | **Not established.** No farmer-specific 2026 exit data exists yet. Democrats did win Senate seats in Iowa and Kansas, and Kansas's winner ran on ending the tariffs | KFF/AP; AP VoteCast; Axios; RTÉ/AFP |
