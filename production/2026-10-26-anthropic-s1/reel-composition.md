# Reel composition: "Inside Anthropic's S-1" (2:50)

The shot-by-shot production script. The voiceover, facts and number plates come from [video-brief.md](./video-brief.md); this file adds how each shot is built. Format: 9:16, 1080×1920, for Instagram Reels and TikTok.

## Global rules (apply to every shot)

| Dimension | House standard |
| --- | --- |
| **Visual** | One object, one event per shot. The subject sits inside the 3:4 grid-crop band (y 240–1680px). No AI-generated text or numbers; every number is a rendered plate (`npm run plate`). |
| **Perspective** | The viewer is an insider: eye level or slightly below, looking up at scale. Never a surveillance angle, never looking down on people. |
| **Lighting** | Dark navy base (#0a1020), gold rim light (#C9A24B), warm practicals. High contrast, no red alarm light, no strobing. |
| **Style** | Cinematic photoreal, shallow depth of field, light film grain, slow and confident. Financial Gurkha's look: navy and gold, serif plates. |
| **Structure / timeline** | Hook in 0–2s → a number every 10–20s → a turn at 0:34 ("the loss isn't what it seems") → payoff → close with a loop back to the opening. |
| **Viral triggers** | Curiosity gap, number reveal, "what most people miss", contrast (loss vs profit), insider access, a rewatch loop. **Tone law:** energy and wonder, never fear or dread. |
| **Camera movement** | Slow, motivated moves (push-in, crane, dolly, parallax). One move per shot, eased in and out. Cut on the beat of the voiceover; let each number land for 1–1.5s of stillness. |

**Audio bed:** a low, warm pulse building to a lift at 1:14 (the cash reveal), a soft drop under the control beat at 1:44, and a resolve at the close. The voiceover is Fish "Anchor News" at 1.25×, ducked −12 dB under music. Subtitles are burned in at y ≈ 1500–1650.
