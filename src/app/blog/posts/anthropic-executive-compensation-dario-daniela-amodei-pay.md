## 3. The Current Structure

Stock-based pay is large company-wide, not just for executives: it was $2.2 billion in the first half of 2026, and it is the biggest item Anthropic adds back to reach its “adjusted” operating income.

![The S-1’s reconciliation of operating loss to Adjusted Operating Income, showing stock-based compensation as the largest add-back](/anthropic-s1/s1-non-gaap-reconciliation.jpg)

| Element | Detail |
| :--- | :--- |
| Base salary | $1.38M (co-founders), $1.25M (CFO); reviewed twice a year |
| Options | Vest monthly over one year |
| RSUs | Vest quarterly after a service start date, and only once the IPO registration is effective |
| Severance (Oct 2026) | 24 months of salary for the co-founders, 12 for the CFO, plus 12 months of vesting acceleration, if terminated without cause |
| Perks | Security, private air for Dario under his security program; $2.5M each paid for the Amodeis’ antitrust (HSR) filing fees |

## 5. The Philosophy Behind It

The filing sets out three rules:
- **Pay targets the 90th percentile** of the AI development market.
- **The same role, level and location gets the same pay.** All seven co-founders are paid equally.
- **Plan design stays simple.** Most pay is equity that vests over years.
