## The 60-Second Version

| Period | Revenue | Growth |
| :--- | ---: | :--- |
| 2023 | $41M | — |
| 2024 | $386M | 9.4× |
| 2025 | $4.59B | 11.9× |
| First half 2026 | **$16.3B** | 14.0× the first half of 2025 |
| Q2 2026 | **$11.6B** | 14.7× Q2 2025; 2.44× Q1 2026 |

1. **Revenue grew about tenfold a year,** then 14× year on year in the first half of 2026.
2. **Growth slowed through 2025, then re-accelerated in 2026,** as the agent products Claude Code and Claude Cowork spread.
3. **Compute cost per revenue dollar fell from $2.51 to $0.48 in six quarters.** That drove the first operating profit: $974 million in Q2 2026.

## 4. How Growth Became Profit

**The turn to profit came from compute getting cheaper per dollar of revenue, not from spending less.**

| Quarter | Compute cost | Compute per $1 of revenue | Operating income (loss) | Margin |
| :--- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Q1 2025 | $944M | $2.51 | $(935)M | −249% |
| Q2 2025 | $1,390M | $1.77 | $(1,200)M | −152% |
| Q3 2025 | $2,047M | $1.48 | $(3,089)M | −224%\* |
| Q4 2025 | $2,945M | $1.44 | $(2,836)M | −139% |
| Q1 2026 | $3,387M | $0.72 | $(1,667)M | −35% |
| **Q2 2026** | **$5,558M** | **$0.48** | **$974M** | **+8.4%** |

\*Includes the $1.5 billion _Bartz_ copyright settlement.

![Compute cost per dollar of revenue falling from $2.51 to $0.48](/anthropic-s1/anthropic-compute-cost-per-revenue-dollar.jpg)

Compute spending still grew **4×** year on year in Q2 2026, while revenue grew **14.7×**. The filing’s own phrasing: revenue “grew 1,368% year over year… compared to compute and infrastructure expenses that grew 300%.”

On Anthropic’s non-GAAP measure, which excludes stock compensation, the adjusted operating margin went from **−221% in Q1 2025 to +19% in Q2 2026**.

![The S-1’s chart: adjusted operating margin rose 240 percentage points, from −221% to 19%](/anthropic-s1/s1-adjusted-operating-margin-chart.jpg)

## 5. What Kind of Revenue It Is

**Most revenue is pay-as-you-go.** Customers pay per token of model use, rather than fixed subscriptions.

| | Consumption | Subscriptions |
| :--- | ---: | ---: |
| 2024 | 75% | 25% |
| 2025 | 83% | 17% |
| H1 2026 | 80% | 20% |

Large customers drive it:
- **Customer count:** customers paying $100,000+ a year grew from about 20 at the end of 2023 to more than **11,900** by September 2026. In 2025 they produced 76% of revenue.
- **Expansion:** existing customers’ spending grew about **9×** in a year (net revenue retention of ~900%).
- **Breadth:** no customer exceeds 10% of revenue. Beyond the large accounts there are about 40 million smaller customers.

![The S-1’s chart of customers paying $100,000 or more a year](/anthropic-s1/s1-customers-100k-chart.jpg)

## 6. What the Filing Says About the Future

The filing is candid:
- **Growth will slow.** It expects the growth rate to decline, “possibly significantly.”
- **New releases drive revenue.** Models released in the prior six months produced over 80% of Q3 2026 revenue.
- **Seasonality matters.** Customer spending fell about 20% over the 2025–26 holiday weeks, an effect that will show more as growth slows.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What was Anthropic’s revenue in 2025?**
Anthropic’s revenue was $4.585 billion in 2025, up 11.9 times from $386 million in 2024, according to its S-1.

**What was Anthropic’s revenue in the second quarter of 2026?**
$11.555 billion, 2.44 times the prior quarter and 14.7 times the $787 million of Q2 2025.

**When did Anthropic become profitable?**
Anthropic reported its first quarterly operating profit in Q2 2026: $974 million, an 8.4% operating margin. It remains loss-making on a net basis because of non-cash fair-value charges on convertible notes.

**Why did Anthropic’s margins improve so fast?**
Because compute got cheaper per unit of revenue. Compute and infrastructure cost fell from $2.51 per revenue dollar in Q1 2025 to $0.48 in Q2 2026, even as compute spending grew fourfold.
