## What happened

September inflation came in hotter than expected — 3.7% year-over-year, up from 3.4% in August and above the 3.6% consensus. Prices rose 0.6% in a single month. And this time the pain landed directly in wallets: real average hourly earnings fell 0.5% on the month (nominal pay rose just 0.1%), the sixth straight month paychecks lost ground to prices.

The driver is no mystery. Gasoline surged 9.1% in September alone — accounting for more than half of the entire monthly increase, according to the Bureau of Labor Statistics. The overall energy index rose 5.2%. This is a conflict-and-supply-disruption story: war-driven oil shocks flowing straight into the price level, with Brent around $106 and WTI near $96.

An honest note: it wasn't *only* energy. Core inflation (excluding food and energy) also ran warm at 2.6% year-over-year, and shelter rose 0.4% — its fastest monthly pace in five months. But energy did the heavy lifting this month, and that matters for what comes next.

## Our take

This looks like an energy shock, not a broad demand spiral — and energy shocks fade. The IMF's October outlook, released yesterday, assumes crude averages about $96 a barrel this year and eases toward roughly $87 in 2027 on the futures path. September saw some of the highest crude prices in recent times; if that peak is behind us, inflation likely begins cooling within a month or two.

Two things worth sitting with. First, even America — the world's biggest crude producer — can't dodge this, because oil is a global market traded in dollars; when the world price spikes, everyone pays it. Second, the Fund's warning lands hardest elsewhere: import-reliant economies, especially less-developed ones, face what the IMF calls "the most difficult configuration" — high energy bills with no tech-boom cushion.

Markets got the message. Indexes ran red across the board, the 10-year Treasury pushed toward 5.3%, and traders repriced the odds of another Fed hike. Thursday brings PPI and retail sales at 8:30 AM — the next read on whether this is spreading or contained.
