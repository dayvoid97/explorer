## The split screen

Wall Street closed a volatile week split sharply down the middle. The **Nasdaq fell 4.5%** — its worst week since June, snapping a four-week winning streak — and the **S&P 500 dropped 2%**, also its worst since June. The **Dow gained 0.7%**, its second straight weekly win, closing at 52,134.63. The VIX pushed north of 20.

Ten days ago, the Nasdaq and S&P both stood at record highs. Then the market started asking an uncomfortable question: what happens to the AI buildout when borrowing costs stay above 5%?

![Nasdaq -4.5%, S&P -2%, Dow +0.7% — the week in one frame](https://financialgurkha.com/news/weekly-wrap-highlight-1.jpg)

## The AI-debt scare

At the center of the rout: CoreWeave, the Nvidia-backed "neocloud" that rents out AI computing power. The stock lost **a quarter of its value this week** and is down more than 65% from its March 2025 IPO peak. Its bonds now yield above 15% — distressed territory. As one tech analyst put it, CoreWeave is "the subprime tranche of the AI trade," selling off first as investors question whether AI returns can cover AI debt.

The contagion spread: the semiconductor ETF fell for a **ninth straight day**, its longest losing streak in two decades, down 7.4% on the week. South Korea's KOSPI plunged 7% overnight Thursday, tripping circuit breakers. Even Jensen Huang's public vote of confidence in CoreWeave on Thursday couldn't hold Friday's 12% drop.

## The bond rally nobody expected

Here's the optimistic half of the story. The 10-year Treasury spiked to **5.33%** after Wednesday's hot CPI — then reversed hard as the tech selloff sent investors into safe havens, ending the week at **5.097%**: bonds' **best week since February**. The 30-year posted its biggest one-week yield decline to 5.436%.

Fed Chair Warsh's overnight remarks did the rest. October hike odds collapsed from **28% to 17%**; December hike odds sit at 89%. The market heard patience — and bought bonds.

![10Y: 5.33% to 5.10% — bonds' best week since February](https://financialgurkha.com/news/weekly-wrap-highlight-2.jpg)

## Energy's quiet surge

While tech burned, energy bid. **Brent crude finished at $109.86**, up 3.7% Friday to its highest since May. WTI gained 4.2% on the week to $97.65. Gold held near $4,186.50, essentially flat. The rotation was textbook: out of leveraged AI, into healthcare, consumer staples, utilities, and energy — all of which attracted buying even on Friday's ugly tape.

## The week ahead

Earnings season goes up a gear. **Tesla reports Tuesday, October 21** — days after its flying-Roadster spectacle — and **Meta follows**, kicking off Magnificent Seven season just as AI spending faces its harshest scrutiny yet. Then October 28: the FOMC decision at 2 PM, with Alphabet reporting after the close.

![What to watch: Tesla Oct 21, FOMC Oct 28, PCE Oct 29](https://financialgurkha.com/news/weekly-wrap-highlight-3.jpg)

## Our take

This was a repricing, not a panic. Breadth wasn't terrible — money didn't leave the market, it moved: from the most leveraged corner of the AI trade into defensives, energy, and bonds. The 10-year's round trip from 5.33% to 5.10% and the collapse in October hike odds say the market is finding its footing, not losing it. The question for next week isn't whether AI demand is real — it's which balance sheets can afford to build it. Tesla and Meta get the first chance to answer.
